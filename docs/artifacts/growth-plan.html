The revenue line

Revenue = publishers × ad requests × fill × CPM × HBDR take

HBDR earns most today as one Google reseller among dozens on large Asian news and streaming sites. Buyers are cutting resellers. The plan moves HBDR from a reseller line to the company that runs the auction, then adds demand that the other resellers do not have.

Three facts the plan rests on

I fetched every publisher's ads.txt and Google's full sellers.json, and read the CTV plan and the repos. Nothing below is an estimate.

  • Fact 1

    HBDR's base is Asian premium news and streaming

    The 309 web domains in headerbiddr.com/sellers.json are mostly large news, portal and video sites in India, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, Taiwan, Malaysia and Turkey. Examples: indiatimes.com, news18.com, bangkokpost.com, detik.com, cnnturk.com, gmanetwork.com, vnexpress.net, jpost.com.

    Five streaming and video apps are already HBDR sellers: ZEE5 (seller 202, ASIA TV USA LTD), Vidio (its Android TV app is listed), Sun NXT (282), MX Player and ShareChat (208).

    Source: https://headerbiddr.com/sellers.json, fetched 2026-10-29: 325 rows, 225 seller IDs, 309 web domains. Detail: docs/research/reseller-map-2026-10-29.md.

  • Fact 3

    Buyers are cutting reseller paths

    The Trade Desk now classes SSP paths as resellers and scores them as less efficient. It steers spend to direct paths (OpenPath) and a curated set of 500 supply routes. Its rules ask for one seller ID per exchange for each site and the same business domain in every sellers.json.

    HeaderBiddr's sellers.json repeats 41 seller IDs, and 14 of them name different companies. 55 publishers carry a HeaderBiddr line that fails the buyer's check.

    Sources: The Trade Desk partner docs, OpenPath inventory best practices; Digiday on Kokai and supply paths (links at the end). HeaderBiddr data: docs/research/ads-txt-audit-2026-10-23.md.

What each move does, what it uses, and the proof

Bet A · Take the auction on sites HBDR already sells

Answers fact 2. A reseller line gets whatever is left after the site's main partner. The company that runs the wrapper sets the auction and the floors.

  • A1

    Primary-partner offer to the top 20 existing publishers

    Offer each publisher HBDR's Prebid.js wrapper and managed Prebid Server on a share of their traffic. HBDR measures revenue per thousand pageviews against their current setup. If HBDR wins, it takes more traffic and becomes the main partner, not one line in fifty.

    Start with sites where HBDR already has a line and a relationship: the 120 sites with a HeaderBiddr Google line are the pool.

    Reuses: wrapper A/B split (packages/edge/src/delivery/lib/ab-test.ts)Reuses: floor rules (packages/console/src/routes/config/floors.tsx)

    Evidence: fact 2. D-029 row 2: HBDR offers Manage Inventory as well as Manage Account.

  • A2

    Clean the supply path buyers check

    Give each of the 41 repeated seller IDs to one company, give the others new IDs, and send the 55 publishers with broken lines their correct line. Until this is done, a buyer that follows The Trade Desk's rules can mark HBDR's path as invalid, and every other move earns less.

    Built: /admin/supply in PR #34Writes: supply_sellers, supply_changes

    Evidence: fact 3; docs/research/ads-txt-audit-2026-10-23.md.

Bet B · A competitor map that feeds sales

Answers facts 1 and 2. The same lookup that showed who sits beside HBDR works on any site in Asia.

  • B1

    Map every site each competitor manages in eight markets

    Take the top sites by traffic in India, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, Taiwan, Malaysia, the Philippines and Turkey. Read each site's ads.txt and name every Google reseller through Google's sellers.json. Load the home page once and record whether it runs Prebid.js and which bidders it calls.

    The result is a sales list: every site that Adpushup, Datawrkz, Streamlyn, Mediology and the others manage, sized by traffic, with what the site runs today.

    Reads: public ads.txt, Google sellers.json, CrUX country rank listsCost: one fetch per site per runRuns on request; no schedule

    Evidence: the method in fact 2 named 74% of 17,009 reseller lines on HBDR's own sites. Tested 2026-10-29.

  • B2

    Prove-it trial: a share of traffic, measured, no contract

    Offer every site from B1 the same test as A1: HBDR runs a share of traffic for a set number of days, and the publisher sees the split result. The share and the length are operator settings that you set, not values in code.

    Evidence: D-029 row 4 (no setup fee, no lock-in); doctrine 14 (settings are operator controls).

  • B3

    Win back the 47,000 past sites

    HBDR has worked with 47,000+ sites and 309 web domains remain in sellers.json. Run B1's lookup over the old list. Keep sites that are still live and above 500,000 monthly pageviews, and show who manages each one now.

    Reads: the legacy site list (hbdrmcm MySQL)

    Evidence: D-027 row 1 (47,000+ sites), row 4 (500,000+ pageviews minimum).

  • B4

    Quarterly "Prebid in Asia" report from the map

    Publish B1's totals by country: share of sites on Prebid.js, the most common bidders, identity modules, and the number of resellers per site. Only HBDR has this table for these markets. It goes out as a Press post and gives sales a reason to call.

    Evidence: /press lists Press-category posts (D-031). Public data only, so no publisher permission is needed.

Bet C · Demand the other resellers do not bring

Answers fact 3. A buyer keeps a path that brings something unique. Video and packaged deals are what HBDR can bring.

  • C1

    CTV charter publishers from the streaming apps HBDR already sells

    The CTV plan starts with cold outreach. ZEE5, Vidio, Sun NXT, MX Player and ShareChat already work with HBDR. Offer them the VAST tag first: no SDK, and it works in any player that accepts a tag URL.

    One code gap blocks billing: the CTV event recorder is still a placeholder, so there are no numbers to invoice yet.

    Reuses: hbdr-ctv /vast/tag, 8 SSP adaptersBlocker: analytics.recordEvent placeholder

    Evidence: fact 1; ~/code/hbdr-ctv/docs/MONETIZATION-PLAN.md §0 and Phase 0. D-033: CTV SSAI is shown as live before launch, so this also makes that claim true.

  • C2

    Curated deals: Asian news by market, topic and event

    Package impressions across HBDR's publishers into deal IDs that DSPs and agency desks buy directly. Examples: Indian cricket during the IPL, Southeast Asia elections, Diwali, Ramadan, Lunar New Year. A deal carries a higher CPM than the open auction, and HBDR sets the deal price.

    hbdr.com gets one buyer page listing markets, formats and monthly volume. A publisher is named there only with their written OK.

    Reuses: prebid-server internal/deals (manager.go)Today: no deals live (D-029 row 9)

    Evidence: fact 3 (buyers favor curated routes); D-029 row 9 (HBDR can access PMP and PG deals).

  • C3

    Article topic labels in the bid request, if news bids less

    First, measure: compare bid rate and CPM on hard-news pages with other pages on the same sites. If news earns clearly less, label each article with its IAB content category and send it in the bid request, so buyers can target topics instead of blocking keywords. The labels also feed C2.

    This is a test of an idea, not a fact. If the gap is small, stop after the measurement.

    Reads: ClickHouse bid_events
Bet D · Terms that win switches

Business terms. Little or no code.

  • D1

    Faster payment for a fee

    Non-Google revenue pays Net 60 today. Offer Net 15 for a fee. For a mid-size publisher, cash in hand sooner is a reason to switch that the resellers in fact 2 do not offer. This needs cash to cover the gap, so it is your call before any work.

    Evidence: D-029 row 4 (Net 30 Google, Net 60 other sources).

  • D2

    Publish the supply path on hbdr.com once A2 is done

    A public page with HBDR's sellers.json, ads.txt lines and the line each publisher adds, read from the same table as /sellers.json. Buyers and publishers can check the path in one place.

    Evidence: D-031, D-034; /sellers.json and /ads.txt built in PR #34.

Left out

Ideas I checked and dropped

Each one fails on a number or on a missing piece.

  • Charge AI crawlers for publishers' pages

    An April 2026 analysis of Cloudflare's pay per crawl put revenue at tens to low hundreds of dollars a month for a site with 1 million pageviews. In July 2026 Cloudflare moved to an experimental pay-per-use model. Too small to move HBDR's revenue.

  • Sell HBDR's stack to other networks under their brand

    Prebid Server phases 7–9 (multi-region, production hardening) are not done (~/code/hbdr-suite/ECOSYSTEM.md). Look again after that.

  • Version 1's site tweaks

    The calculator email, waitlist field, booking link, referral page and /technology page stay as small tasks. None of them changes a term of the revenue line alone.
