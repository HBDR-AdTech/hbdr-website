How one impression is sold.

Header bidding asks every partner at once while the page loads. Bids that miss the timeout are dropped, so a slow bidder never holds the page.

Everyone bids at once

The page sends one request to every partner in parallel instead of offering the slot to networks one after another.

The highest price wins

Bids go to your ad server together, so the best offer for that impression is the one that serves.

Timeouts protect page speed

A partner that answers too late is skipped for that impression. Readers never wait on a slow bidder.
